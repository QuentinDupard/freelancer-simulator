Calculation Formulas by Structure

SASU

President = assimilé salarié (general social security regime)

grossSalary = revenue - expenses
charges = grossSalary × 0.82
netSalary = grossSalary / 1.82
incomeTax = netSalary × progressiveRate
disposable = netSalary - incomeTax
Employer charges~54% of net
Employee charges~28% of net
Total charges~82% of net
Health / Maternity13%
Retirement (base)17.75%
Retirement (compl.)~8%
Family allowances5.25%
CSG + CRDS9.7%
UnemploymentN/A (no rights)

PASS 2026: €47,100/year. No minimum charges if no salary. Dividends taxed at flat 30% (PFU) with no social charges.

EURL

Manager = TNS (Travailleur Non-Salarié)

grossSalary = revenue - expenses
charges = grossSalary × 0.45
netSalary = grossSalary / 1.45
incomeTax = netSalary × progressiveRate
disposable = netSalary - incomeTax
Total charges~45% of net
Health / Maternity0.5–6.5%
Retirement (base)17.75%
Retirement (compl.)~8-9%
Family allowances0–3.1%
Disability / Death1.3%
CSG + CRDS9.7%

Minimum annual charges ~€1,224 even with no revenue. Dividends above 10% of capital subject to social charges. 26% abattement on new assiette (reform 2025).
